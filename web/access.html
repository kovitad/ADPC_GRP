Before your first sign-in

Get access to GRP

GRP does not support self-registration. Your Hub administrator must approve access using the same verified work email as your SERVIR account.

  1. 1

    Ask your Hub administrator

    They add your exact work email, Hub, and role to the GRP member list.

  2. 2

    Confirm your SERVIR account

    If you do not have one, the SIG account team sends the SERVIR invitation.

  3. 3

    Return and sign in

    Your first approved sign-in securely links your identity to the member record.
